# Brand — from the final moodboard

**Statement:** "I have been inside the problem. Now I help founders decide
what to do next."

## Logo — "Ap"
Ap stands for Anjan Prasad. The A is straight, steady and off-white (navy on
light backgrounds); the p is slanted and gold, the signature. One logo,
everywhere. Files: `public/brand/01_Primary` (SVG + PNG), one-colour
variants, favicons, social profile images, banners, email and document marks.
Component: `src/components/brand/ApMark.tsx` (`tone="light" | "dark"`).

## Colours (in this order of use)
| Colour | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy | `#0B1A2E` | Main colour: trust (55%) |
| Off-white | `#F4F1EB` | Backgrounds: calm (25%) |
| Gold | `#CDB27A` | The p, buttons, key details (15%) |
| Soft blue | `#A9BDD1` | A quiet extra (5%) |
| Deep gold | `#A5874A` | Gold on light backgrounds |
| Slate | `#4C6B8A` | Labels and secondary text on light |

## Fonts
Instrument Serif for big headlines (italic for the word we want to stand
out); Geist for paragraphs, menus and small labels (labels in gold/slate).

## Photos
Real photos of Anjan: natural light, plain background, relaxed pose. No stock
photos, no stage shots, no crossed arms.

## Voice
Always "I", never "Anjan does". Short sentences, simple words, no big promises.
